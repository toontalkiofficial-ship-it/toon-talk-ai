# Toon Talk AI Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

Toon Talk AI lets users request AI-generated images and videos.

## Information processed
- **Prompts and generation requests:** Text that a user enters is sent to Pollinations to generate requested media.
- **Pollinations API key:** The user-provided key is stored in the app's private preferences on that device and is sent to Pollinations in an authorization header when making generation requests. The app developer should never ask users to send their key to the developer.
- **Generated media:** Generated image/video responses are displayed or played on the device. The app does not operate its own media-generation server.
- **Advertising:** The app includes Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, and usage information according to Google's applicable policies and the user's device settings.

## Third-party services
Requests to Pollinations are subject to Pollinations' terms and privacy practices. Ads are subject to Google's advertising and privacy terms. Review the providers' current policies before public release.

## Storage and sharing
The app stores the Pollinations key locally in app-private storage. Users should clear the key before sharing their device. Generated content may be sent to the third-party AI service and should not include confidential information.

## Children and content
The app is not represented as designed specifically for children. The publisher must set the correct target audience and implement all applicable content and advertising safeguards before publication.
